## Scheduled Maintenance on Dec 24 (Done)

We recently detected an abnormal rise of frame error rate on the network interface card. A maintenance will be schedule shortly to tackle the problem.

From 16:00 to 20:00 on Dec 24, the HTTP service will be redirected to nearby mirrors, whilst the rsync service will be unavailable.

After the maintenance is completed, or if there are unexpected changes or extensions to the maintenance time, we will update this notice if possible.

May you have a great time with free software.

Update 21:25: The server has been started and the repos are starting to sync. Some pages of the HTTP service are degraded, this will be fixed soon.
