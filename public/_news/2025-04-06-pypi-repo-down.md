## PyPI 仓库故障

由于 PyPI 仓库体积增长速度超出预期，导致其阵列空间耗尽，目前 PyPI 的仓库请求暂时被重定向至[北京外国语大学开源软件镜像站](https://mirrors.bfsu.edu.cn/pypi/)。

我们计划在近期研究设置缓存式部分同步，之后再恢复 PyPI 仓库服务，非常抱歉给您的使用造成不便。

愿您与自由软件度过愉快的时光。

2025 年 4 月 6 日

## PyPI Repository Service Interruption

Due to the growth in size of the PyPI repository beyond our expectation, the repository has exhausted its storage. Currently, all requests to the PyPI repository has been redirected to [Beijing Foreign Studies University Open-Source Mirrors](https://mirrors.bfsu.edu.cn/pypi/).

We will be trying to deploy a partial syncing cache of PyPI soon, and will resume to serve the repository after that. Sorry for the inconvenience.

May you have great time with free software.

Apr 6, 2025
